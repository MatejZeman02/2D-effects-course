# Where the photos come from

`coffee.png` is the coffee photo that [scikit-image](https://scikit-image.org)
ships as `skimage.data.coffee`, taken by Rachel Michetti and released under
CC0. `build/lesson_03_diagrams.py` enlarges it twice, from 600 × 400 to
1200 × 800, with Lanczos resampling, so that the timing cells of the lesson
have enough pixels to tell the kernels apart. The script writes it again on
every run.

| File | Picture | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `coffee.png` | a cup of coffee, Rachel Michetti (`skimage.data.coffee`), enlarged twice | CC0 |

Every other picture here is drawn by the diagram script.

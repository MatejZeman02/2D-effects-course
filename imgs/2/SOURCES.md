# Where the photos come from

Four photos here are not drawn by `build/lesson_02_diagrams.py`. They come
from Wikimedia Commons, each cropped to the largest square around its centre
and scaled to 480 × 480 with Lanczos resampling. The diagram script puts them
above the lesson's own Voronoi noise in `voronoi_nature.jpg`, with each
photo's author and licence under it, and writes that picture again on every
run.

| File | Picture | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `giraffe.jpg` | [Giraffe skin](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Giraffe_skin_(2768318773).jpg) | flowcomm | [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0) |
| `dragonfly_wing.jpg` | [Vein pattern on a dragonfly wing](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vein_pattern_on_a_dragonfly_wing.jpg) | Patilssp | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0) |
| `dried_mud.jpg` | [Cracks on dry ground, dried muds](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cracks_on_dry_ground,_Dried_muds.jpg) | Novoklimov | [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| `basalt.jpg` | [Giant's Causeway, hexagons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Giant%27s_Causeway-_Hexagons.jpg) | David Singleton | [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0) |

Every other picture here is drawn by the diagram script.

# Where the photos come from

`chelsea.png` is the cat photo that [scikit-image](https://scikit-image.org)
ships as `skimage.data.chelsea`, taken by Stefan van der Walt and released
under CC0. `build/lesson_05_diagrams.py` enlarges it twice, from 451 × 300 to
902 × 600, with Lanczos resampling, and writes it again on every run.

| File | Picture | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `chelsea.png` | Chelsea the cat, Stefan van der Walt (`skimage.data.chelsea`), enlarged twice | CC0 |

Every other picture here is drawn by the diagram script.
